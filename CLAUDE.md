## Learned rules
- Keep all project 3D assets in canonical glTF format; convert any other model format to glTF rather than mixing formats.
- No exceptions to the stimulus system: every effect (on objects, NPCs, the player, blocks, liquids) goes through Stimulate and receptrons, never a special-case check at a hit site or in a tick.
